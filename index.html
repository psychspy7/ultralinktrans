HOW THE SIGNAL MOVES03

A tiny protocol inside sound.

01

Frame the text

UTF-8 text becomes a versioned binary packet with payload length and CRC-16 integrity check.

02

Modulate bits

Each bit becomes one of two high-frequency carriers: 18.0 kHz for 0 and 19.4 kHz for 1.

03

Recover locally

The decoder detects the preamble, measures both carrier frequencies, reconstructs bytes, and validates the checksum.

REAL-WORLD NOTE04

Near-ultrasonic, not magic.

Phone speakers and microphones vary. Some devices heavily filter frequencies above ~18 kHz, and some people or animals may still hear them. For reliable tests, keep devices close, use a high media volume, avoid noisy rooms, and prefer WAV when possible.
